# Brag Plan: ProofLayer

## What is this app?
ProofLayer is an enterprise AI gateway delivering sub-10ms deterministic threat interception, Ed25519-signed cryptographic audit ledgers, Bengali & English multi-lingual safety defense, and dual-key quorum approvals for autonomous agent workflows.

## The angle
Autonomous AI agents are executing code and accessing critical databases, but enterprises cannot prove what their agents did or whether malicious prompts breached containment. ProofLayer turns safety from a vague promise into mathematical, tamper-evident cryptographic proof: 541 live audit events cryptographically chained, 90 zero-day attacks neutralized in under 6ms, and 2,793 curated bilingual benchmarks with zero data leaks.
